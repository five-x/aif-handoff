## Trusted-State Rule

Trusted audit state is not allowed to come from raw report prose alone.

- Ledger evidence may influence trusted state only through manifest-bound `auditEvidenceUnits` with ledger requirements enabled in `evaluateTaskCompletionEvidence()` and `validateAuditReportArtifact()`.
- Committed blob validation may influence trusted state only through `verifyAuditArtifactLifecycle()` and persisted lifecycle-valid `validationDetails`.
- Raw report text may influence diagnostics, repair guidance, review comments, and operator display, but it cannot satisfy trusted completion or trusted synthesis without ledger evidence and committed blob/lifecycle proof.

Concrete trust proof is centralized in [packages/shared/src/taskCompletionEvidence.ts:1321](../../packages/shared/src/taskCompletionEvidence.ts): `hasTrustedAuditArtifactProof()` requires validator `ok`, valid manifest, trusted source classification, `requireAuditLedgerEvidence === true`, lifecycle `ok`, `artifact_state_valid`, and committed validation `ok`.
